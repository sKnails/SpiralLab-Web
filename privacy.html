Overview

Photo Fisher is a photo and video navigator designed to help you find moments in your camera roll quickly. This policy explains the current V1 privacy intent in plain language.

Photo Fisher does not require an account and is designed to keep the browsing experience on your device. We do not sell your photos.

Camera-roll access

Photo Fisher can access your device camera roll only after you grant the operating system permission. You can manage or revoke that permission in your device settings.

In V1, the app is intended to use that access to help you navigate photos and videos locally. Photo Fisher does not access content from other apps.

What V1 does not do

✓

No account: V1 does not ask you to create or sign in to an account.

✓

No backend: V1 is not intended to send your camera-roll content to a Photo Fisher server.

✓

No uploads: your photos and videos are not uploaded by Photo Fisher in this V1 intent.

✓

No analytics: V1 has no planned analytics collection.

✓

No advertising SDK: V1 has no planned advertising SDK.

✓

No sale of photos: Photo Fisher does not sell your photos or videos.

Sharing

If you choose to share a photo, video, or other item, the system share action is user initiated. Your operating system and the app you choose to share with may process the item according to their own privacy policies.

Photo Fisher does not start a share action without your choice.

Paid unlocks

A paid unlock may be added in a future release. When that happens, Google Play or the App Store may process purchase, payment, and transaction information under the applicable store’s terms and privacy policy. Photo Fisher does not receive your full payment-card details through the store purchase flow.

This section must be reviewed against the final purchase implementation and store declarations before a paid unlock is released.

Your choices

You can choose whether to grant camera-roll permission, change that permission later, and decide which items to share. You can also remove the app using your device’s normal uninstall tools.

If the V1 behavior changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy and the relevant store disclosures should be updated before that version is released.
