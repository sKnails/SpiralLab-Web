How it works

Cast a little wider. Reel in a lot sooner.

Photo Fisher keeps the journey simple, so the search feels more like browsing a memory and less like managing a database.

Built with a privacy-first point of view ↗
  1. 01

    Give it permission

    Let Photo Fisher access your camera roll when you choose. The app starts with your permission, not a guess.

  2. 02

    Cast a filter

    Use the visual navigator to narrow the water and move toward the memory you have in mind.

  3. 03

    Reel it in

    When you find the moment, take the next step yourself — including a system share action you initiate.

Privacy-first

Your memories stay in your waters.

Photo Fisher V1 is designed around local camera-roll access after you grant permission. No account. No backend. No uploads. No analytics or advertising SDK in this V1 intent.
